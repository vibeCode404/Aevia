Privacy Policy — Aevia

Last updated: October 2026

Aevia helps you record and preserve family and home knowledge — stories, instructions, memories, and practical details — using voice, text, photos, and video. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Your content never leaves your phone. Aevia has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. Everything you record is stored only on your device. We, the developers, cannot see, access, or recover it.

What we collect

Nothing. Aevia does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server, because there is no server. There is no sign-in and no account.

What stays on your device

  • Your entries, voice recordings, photos, videos, medicines list, and deleted items (Trash) — all stored only on your device.
  • Your settings, including your home-country prompt pack, reminder time, and text size.

If you delete the app, all of this is deleted with it. There is no cloud copy to restore from.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Microphone — to record voice entries and for voice-to-text dictation.
  • Camera — to take photos and record video for your entries.
  • Notifications — for the optional daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device only.
  • File access (via system pickers and the share sheet) — only when you choose to import a backup or export/share one. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Voice-to-text: one honest caveat

Dictation uses your device's built-in speech recognition service (on Android, Google's speech service). That service may process audio on the device or, for some languages, over the internet, under that service's own privacy policy. Aevia itself does not send your recordings anywhere — we have no server to send them to — and your saved recordings stay on your device regardless.

Text-to-speech

The "listen" feature reads prompts aloud using your device's on-device text-to-speech. No audio is sent anywhere.

Import and export

Backup, family-package sharing, and the emergency package are entirely under your control: a file is created only when you tap export/share, and it goes only where you choose to send it. The emergency package is encrypted with a PIN you set; if you forget that PIN, its contents cannot be recovered — by you or by us.

Premium purchase

If you buy the one-time premium unlock, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Aevia is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
